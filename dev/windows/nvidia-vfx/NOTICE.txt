Tigerest Theater: NVIDIA Video Effects runtime for frame generation

The DLLs in vfg\nvidia are unmodified files from NVIDIA's nvidia-vfx 0.2.0.0
package for Windows x64 (NVIDIA Video Effects SDK runtime):

  NVVideoEffects.dll, NVCVImage.dll, nvVFXVideoFrameGeneration.dll,
  cudart64_12.dll, nvngxruntime.dll

They are Copyright NVIDIA Corporation and are distributed under NVIDIA's
license terms, not under Tigerest Theater's license. The terms that came with
the package are included in this folder:

  NVIDIA-Software-License-Agreement-2025.05.05.pdf
  product-specific-terms-for-nvidia-ai-products-2025.05.05.pdf
  NVIDIA-Open-Model-License-Agreements-24-10-2025.pdf
  THIRD_PARTY.md, ThirdPartyLicenses_VFX_SDK.txt

Tigerest Theater loads these files only for NVIDIA frame generation (VFG), on
NVIDIA GeForce RTX 40 or 50 series graphics cards. Deleting the vfg\nvidia
folder disables that feature and nothing else.

The package's SHA-256, its download address on NVIDIA's package index and the
hash of every file are recorded in runtime-lock.json.
https://pypi.nvidia.com/nvidia-vfx/
